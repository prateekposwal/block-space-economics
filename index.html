Bitcoin block space, live

Bitcoin Sahi is an autonomous research engine measuring Bitcoin’s fee market, mempool, and block space in real time. Every bar above is one Bitcoin block — the floating particles are transactions waiting in the mempool.

Live right now: the fastest and economy fee in satoshis per virtual byte (sat/vB), how many transactions are waiting to be confirmed, and the current block height. A one-vbyte economy-rate transaction costs a fraction of a US cent — or more, when fee pressure rises.

Live network state

  • Fees — fastest and economy rates in sat/vB, and what a typical transaction costs in dollars.
  • Mempool — how many transactions are competing for the next block.
  • Block height — the current tip of the chain; each bar in the skyline is one block from the last 24 hours.

Latest research

Bitcoin Resource Accounting — open research on the long-lived resources Bitcoin consumes (replicated storage, UTXO state, validation, relay, bandwidth) and how much of each cost the fee market internalizes. Storage — the Storage Cost Coverage Ratio (SCCR) — is Metric #1, measured and reproducible.
