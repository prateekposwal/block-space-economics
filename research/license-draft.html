1. Recommendation

ArtifactRecommended licenseWhy
Code (tools/, research scripts, reproduction kit)MITStandard, permissive, maximal reuse — the reproducibility claim depends on others *running* the code. MIT removes every friction point.
Paper (working-paper.md/.tex, research/*.md analysis docs)CC BY 4.0Standard for scholarly text; requires attribution (preserves the "Prateek Poswal / Bitcoin Sahi Research" credit) while allowing reuse/derivation.
arXiv submission licenseCC BY 4.0 (arXiv option)arXiv's non-exclusive license is the fallback; CC BY 4.0 is the modern default for maximal reuse and is what the paper should carry.

What this changes in the repo:

  • LICENSE (currently a stub) becomes the MIT text for code.

(the paper itself licenses its text; the repo LICENSE covers code).

replaced with the chosen license notice.

arXiv angle: arXiv requires a license choice at upload. Selecting CC BY 4.0

there is independent of the repo LICENSE but should match the paper's notice.
