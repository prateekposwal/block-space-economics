BSAHI Research

Bitcoin Resource Accounting — the research program of Bitcoin Sahi: a complete accounting system for every long-lived resource Bitcoin consumes (replicated storage, UTXO state, validation, relay, bandwidth), quantifying how much of each cost the fee market internalizes. SCCR (storage) is Metric #1 — the first measured member of the RIR family; the rest are research hypotheses. Open research on block space economics — the fee market, mempool dynamics, and the permanent cost of data storage.

Definitions

Self-contained definitions for the terms used across this library.

SCCR — Storage Cost Coverage Ratio
Transaction fees paid (USD) divided by the modeled 10-year storage cost borne by full nodes (USD) at a node count N. A ratio below 1.0 means fees do not cover the modeled cost of storing the data across the network.
N — node count
The number of full nodes bearing the storage cost. Primary source: a lower-bound census via Bitcoin Core getnodeaddresses; the canonical constant is 32,000.
T — storage time horizon
The assumed lifetime over which stored data is borne by nodes. T = 10 years.
L_net — modeled network storage cost
The modeled per-block storage cost across the network, in USD. At N=32,000 and T=10 yr it is $5,627.80 per block (model-spec v2.1.0).
