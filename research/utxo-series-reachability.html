Verdict

Build-or-document: DOCUMENTED. Row 6 historical stays D; VCI state leg stays estimate-table. The pre-2016 continuous UTXO-count series requires one of:

  1. A synced local Bitcoin Core node (the ready path — the census node is ~320K blocks behind; once synced, gettxoutsetinfo captures give a true primary series going forward, and per-block nTx-derived deltas cover the back-fill). This is the R5 node-sync decision, already deferred.
  2. A blockchair full block scan from a session/host tolerant of its rate limits (~10K requests), computing Σ(outputs − non-coinbase inputs) per block.

No fabricated UTXO numbers exist in the repo.
