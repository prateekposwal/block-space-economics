Regime Event — SCCR 0.16 → 0.44: The Fee Market's Storage Coverage, Measured Live

Published 2026-08-21 · Source: live /data/sccr_history.json + /data/sccr_latest.json (spec v2.1.0, N=32K lower-bound census, T=10yr)

SCCR rose from ~0.16 to ~0.44 in roughly 4–5 days — the fee market's storage coverage doubled-then-nearly-tripled, measured live on real blocks.

"Storage Cost Coverage Ratio" = transaction fees ÷ modeled 10-year storage cost across the real 32K-node census. Below 1.0 = fees do not cover what the data costs the network. It is a live, dated quantity — and it just moved sharply.

Why it matters (factual reading, no spin)

  • This is the first sustained break above the ~0.22–0.29 band that characterized the Aug 02–15 record, into a new, higher regime. That band was the historical baseline for the unpriced-storage-externality thesis; today's reading is material evidence the gap is fee-market-driven and time-varying, not a fixed constant.
  • All figures reference the real record: data/sccr_history.json (14 points, 08-02→08-21) and data/sccr_latest.json (avg 0.443333, below_1x 128/133 = 96.24%).
