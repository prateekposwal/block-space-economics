Bitcoin's fee market is the most sophisticated congestion pricing mechanism in the digital asset world. It allocates ~7 MB of block space per day among thousands of competing transactions.

But it only prices **congestion** — not **permanence**.

Every transaction is stored by ~27,800 nodes forever. The fee is paid once. The storage persists for years.

Our Storage Cost Coverage Ratio measures this gap.

Current ratio: 0.0149 — meaning fees cover 1.5% of 10-year storage cost.
